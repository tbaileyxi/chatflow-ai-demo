# Side Huddle — Alumni Chapter & Fan Club Playbook

_Last updated: 2026-07-28. Kickoff (CFB Week 1) ≈ Aug 29. NFL Week 1 ≈ Sept 10._

---

## 1. Who you're actually emailing (three different people)

| Audience | What they're measured on | What you are to them | Killer objection |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alumni association / chapter relations staff** (university employee) | Engagement %, event turnout, dues renewal, donor pipeline | A number they can report upward | "Is this gambling?" — instant no if unclear |
| **Chapter president / watch-party lead** (volunteer alum) | Turnout, chapter treasury | A fundraiser with a watch party attached | "I don't have time" |
| **Fan club / Facebook group admin** (unpaid volunteer) | Status, not doing work | Free content staff | "You're poaching my members" |

Write three different emails. Do not send one email to all three.

---

## 2. Defuse the gambling objection in line one

A university employee will not forward anything that smells like betting. Every email to a
school-affiliated address must say, before anything else:

> Chips are virtual, there is no cash-out, and no real money changes hands. The app is rated 13+.

Verify this phrasing matches the current App Store listing before it goes out at volume.

---

## 3. The incentive stack (real numbers)

Pick two. Not six.

**Free — costs nothing marginal**
- A named chapter room, set up by you, with their logo and chapter name already in it.
- Chapter leaderboard across the season.
- Priority support line (one text thread with you).

**Status — for the person**
- "Founding Chapter" badge in-app + listed on the site.
- Chapter named in launch posts.

**Proof — for the boss**
- Monthly one-pager: members activated, gamedays active, messages sent, picks made.
- This is disproportionately powerful vs. what it costs you to generate. Alumni staff have a
  nationals office or a board to justify budget to. Hand them the slide.

**Competition — makes them recruit for you**
- Inter-chapter pick'em with a season trophy. A chapter that doesn't play loses to the chapter
  that does. This is the only incentive that spreads on its own.

**Money — the real unlock**
- Team sponsor slots are live: **$250/mo single team**, $650 (3), $1,200 (6), $1,800 (10+),
  month-to-month, one active sponsor per team.
- If a chapter brings in the local business that takes their team's slot (the watch-party bar, an
  alum-owned restaurant, a local insurance agency), **the chapter treasury keeps 40% recurring**
  — ~$100/mo, ~$500 across a season, renewing.
- That converts the ask from "please do me a favor" into "here's a revenue line," using the
  pricing that already exists.

**Rule: money goes to the organization, status goes to the person.** A per-signup bounty paid to a
university employee or a nonprofit board member is a conflict-of-interest problem, and it will
quietly kill yeses you never hear about.

---

## 4. Ladder the ask

Cold email should ask for the thing that costs them zero work.

1. **Ask 1 (cold):** "Can I stand up your chapter's room and send you the flyer?" — a yes with no work attached.
2. **Ask 2:** one watch party as a pilot. QR flyer on the tables.
3. **Ask 3:** newsletter mention + chapter-wide push.
4. **Ask 4:** inter-chapter league, which recruits the next chapters for you.

"Tell your whole membership about my app" is too big for a first email and is why cold sends die.

---

## 5. Ship the work already done

Every send includes, as attachments or links — never as a request:

- Finished Facebook/newsletter post copy (2 versions, short + long)
- A graphic sized for FB and IG story
- A QR flyer, PDF, with their chapter name already on it
- A pre-built room link with their chapter named
- One line for the group's pinned post

If the director has to write anything, it does not happen.

---

## 6. Where to find them

- University alumni association chapter directories (mostly public, with contact emails)
- Official pro-team "fan club" and "official watch party bar" lists
- Facebook groups: `[Team] fans of [City]`, `[School] Alumni of [City]`
- Meetup, and team subreddit sidebars
- The watch-party **bar** is often the better first contact than the chapter: it has both the
  local sponsor money and the turnout motive.

## 8. Email copy

See the three variants and follow-up sequence in the chat transcript for 2026-07-28, reproduced
below.

### A. Chapter president / watch-party lead — fundraiser-forward

**Subject lines**
- Watch party idea for [Chapter] — plus a small revenue line
- [City] [Mascot] chapter — something for gameday
- Free gameday tool for [Chapter], and $100/mo for the treasury

**Body**

> Hi [Name] — I'm Ty, [School] '[YY], and I built Side Huddle: a private group chat for gameday
> where live scores, plays, and a free pick'em run inside the thread. Chips are virtual, there's
> no cash-out and no real money involved — it's rated 13+.
>
> I'd like [Chapter] to have a room for the season. It's free, and I'll set it up myself with
> your chapter name on it — you don't have to build anything.
>
> There's also a revenue piece if you want it. Each team on Side Huddle has one exclusive local
> sponsor slot, $250/month. If your chapter brings in the business that takes [Team]'s slot —
> your watch-party bar is the obvious one — the chapter keeps 40% of it, recurring, straight to
> the treasury.
>
> Worth 10 minutes before kickoff? Or if it's easier, say the word and I'll send the room link
> and a QR flyer for your first watch party, and you can decide after you've seen it.
>
> Ty

### B. Alumni association / chapter relations staff — engagement-metrics-forward

**Subject lines**
- Gameday engagement pilot for [School] chapters — before kickoff
- Reaching [School] alumni where Facebook reach has died
- 10 minutes: chapter engagement tool, free, no gambling component

**Body**

> Hi [Name] — quick and specific, since I know August is your busiest month.
>
> I'm Ty, [School] '[YY]. I built Side Huddle, a private gameday group chat for alumni: live
> scores and plays run inside the thread, plus a free pick'em. To be direct about the thing you'd
> ask me second — chips are virtual, there is no cash-out, no real money moves, and the app is
> rated 13+. There is no wagering component.
>
> The reason I'm writing you and not just the chapters: Facebook reach for chapter pages is gone,
> and email open rates keep sliding. Gameday is the one moment your alumni are already paying
> attention. A chapter room is a place to reach them that isn't a newsletter.
>
> What I'm asking for is one chapter as a pilot — not a system-wide rollout. I'll stand up the
> room, send the post copy and a QR flyer with the chapter's name on it, and after the first month
> I'll send you a one-pager: members activated, gamedays active, messages sent. If the numbers
> aren't worth showing your board, we stop and you've spent nothing.
>
> Which chapter would you point me at? [City] or [City] would be my guess.
>
> Ty

### C. Fan club / Facebook group admin — free-content-forward

**Subject lines**
- Free gameday thing for [Group] — no work for you
- Something for your gameday thread, [Name]
- For [Group] — I'll do the setup, you just pin it

**Body**

> Hey [Name] — I run Side Huddle, a gameday group chat with live scores, plays, and a free pick'em
> in the thread. Virtual chips only, no real money, 13+.
>
> Not asking you to move your group anywhere. The group stays where it is — this is just the
> gameday thread, live, without the Facebook comment section. Most admins use it *for* the group,
> not instead of it.
>
> I'll do all of it: I'll set up the room with [Group]'s name, and send you the post copy, the
> graphic, and a link. You pin it or you don't.
>
> If it works out, you'd be a Founding Group — name in the app and on the site, and first look at
> what we ship next.
>
> Ty

### Follow-ups

**+4 days (all three)**
> Ty here again, on the gameday room for [Chapter/Group]. No pressure either way — but I'm setting
> chapters up in order and kickoff is [X] weeks out. If it's a no, tell me and I'll stop; if it's
> a "maybe later," I'll check back in October.

**+10 days, only if they showed any interest**
> Last one from me. [Other chapter] is running theirs at their first watch party on [date] — happy
> to send you what their numbers look like afterward if you'd rather see it work before you commit.

**Rule:** two follow-ups, then stop. Log every dead one so you don't re-pitch in November.
